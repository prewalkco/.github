# Prewalk

**Prewalk is 360° virtual tour software for real estate and architecture.** It turns 360° photos and equirectangular 3D renders into tours that buyers walk room by room from one link, in the browser on phones, computers and Meta Quest. Nothing to install.

🌐 [prewalk.co](https://prewalk.co) · 📚 [Guides](https://prewalk.co/blog) · ❓ [FAQ](https://prewalk.co/faq) · ✉️ hello@prewalk.co

## Who it is for

Real estate agents and developers, architects, CGI and archviz studios, and 360° photographers who want fast, sharp tours they can share as a link or embed on a website.

## How it works

1. Upload equirectangular (2:1) panoramas from any 360° camera or 3D program, up to 16K (16384 × 8192).
2. Connect rooms by dragging a scene onto the 360° view, then set each room's opening view.
3. Add info points, labels, image and video cards, background music or positioned 3D sound.
4. Share one link (with WhatsApp and LinkedIn previews and a QR code) or embed the tour on any website.

Tiled loading shows a blurred preview first and sharpens the part the viewer looks at, so 16K tours stay fast. Top-bottom stereo renders show real depth in VR.

## What Prewalk is not

Prewalk does not create 3D scans or measurements. If you need those, a scanning platform is the better fit.
